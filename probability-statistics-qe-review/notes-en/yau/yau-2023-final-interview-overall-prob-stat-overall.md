## Question 1

^yau-2023-final-interview-overall-prob-stat-overall-q1

> Corresponding solution: [Reference solution for Question 1](../../solutions/yau/yau-2023-final-interview-overall-prob-stat-overall-solutions.md#^yau-2023-final-interview-overall-prob-stat-overall-s1).

Suppose that $X_{1}, X_{2}, \cdots$ are i.i.d. random variables such that $\mathbb{P}(X_{i} =$ $\begin{array}{r}{1) = \operatorname{P}(X_{i} = - 1) = \frac{1}{2}} \end{array}$ . Take some $a \in(0, 1)$

(1) Prove or disprove that the distribution function of $\begin{array}{r}{\xi = \sum_{k = 1}^{\infty} a^{k} X_{k}} \end{array}$ is continuous.

(2) For $\begin{array}{r}{a = \frac{1}{2}} \end{array}$ , find the distribution of $\xi.$

(3) For $\begin{array}{r}{a = \frac{1}{3}} \end{array}$ , find the distribution of $\xi$ .

## Question 2

^yau-2023-final-interview-overall-prob-stat-overall-q2

> Corresponding solution: [Reference solution for Question 2](../../solutions/yau/yau-2023-final-interview-overall-prob-stat-overall-solutions.md#^yau-2023-final-interview-overall-prob-stat-overall-s2).

Let $F_{2} = \{0, 1\}$ be the number field with two elements and $F_{2}^{d}$ be the d-dimensional vector space over $F_{2}$ . Let $t \leq d$ and $X_{k}, k = 1, 2, \cdots, t,$ be i.i.d random variables uniformly distributed on $F_{2}^{d}$

(1) Find the probability that $X_{1}, X_{2}, \cdots, X_{t}$ are linearly independent (as vectors in $F_{2}^{d})$

(2) Give a positive lower bound (independent of $t,$ and as large as you can) of the above probability.
