1. Let $Z$ be a standard exponential random variable, namely $P(Z \in dt) = e^{-t}dt$. Define $\{Z\}$ and $[Z]$ as the fractional part and integer part of $z$ respectively. Prove that $\{Z\}$ and $[Z]$ are independent and find their distributions respectively.

2. Let $X$ and $Y$ have a bivariate normal distribution with zero means, variances $\sigma^2$ and $\tau^2$, respectively, and correlation coefficient $\rho$. Determine $E[X|X + Y]$.

3. Two table-tennis players, A and B, compete, and A is known to be the stronger player. Which format favors A: best-of-three or best-of-five?
