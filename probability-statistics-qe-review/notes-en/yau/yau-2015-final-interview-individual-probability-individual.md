## Probability and statistics interview questions (individual)

1. Let $\xi_{i}(i = 1, 2, \cdots)$ be i.i.d. random variables with mean ${\mathcal{\mu}}.$ and variance $\sigma$. Let N be a positive-integer-valued random variable with mean $m$ and variance θ, independent of $(\xi_{i}, i =$ $1, 2, \cdots)$. Determine the mean and variance of $\begin{array}{r}{X = \sum_{i = 1}^{N} \xi_{i}} \end{array}$.

2. If X, Y, and Z are independent random variables, each uniformly distributed on [0,1], prove that $W =(XY)^{Z}$ is also uniformly distributed on [0,1].

3. If $E[Y | X] \ = \X$ and $E[X | Y] ~ = ~ Y$, prove that $Y =$ $X(a.s.)$.
