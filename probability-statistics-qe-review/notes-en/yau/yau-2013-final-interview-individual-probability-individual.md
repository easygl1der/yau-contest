## 2013 Probability and Statistics Individual Final-Interview Problems

1. Players A and B each contribute 100 yuan to play a fair game consisting of repeated tosses of a fair coin. A wins a toss on heads and B wins on tails. They agree that the first player to win three tosses receives the full 200-yuan stake. Play is interrupted after the third toss, when A has won twice and B once. How should the 200-yuan stake be divided fairly?

2. Let X and Y be independent, and suppose the distribution of the bivariate random vector $Z =(X, Y)$ is rotation-invariant; that is, OZ has the same distribution as Z for every orthogonal matrix O. Prove that X and Y are Gaussian random variables with mean zero and a common variance.

3. Consider the simple linear regression model

$$
Y_{k} = aX_{k} + \epsilon_{k}, k = 1, 2, \dots, n,
$$

where $\{\epsilon_k:k = 1,2,\dots,n,\}$ is an i.i.d. sequence of $N(0,\sigma^2)$ errors and $\{X_{k}:k = 1,2,\dots,n,\}$ is a deterministic, designed regressor sequence. Give an estimation method and determine the estimator's mean and variance.
