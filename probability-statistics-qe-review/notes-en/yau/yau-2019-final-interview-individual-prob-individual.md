> Corresponding solution: [Reference solution for Question 1](../../solutions/yau/yau-2019-final-interview-individual-prob-individual-solutions.md#^yau-2019-final-interview-individual-prob-individual-s1).

Let $p \geq 1$ and $f, g \in L^{p}[0, 1]$ such that $\begin{array}{r}{\int_{0}^{1} g(y) dy = 0} \end{array}$ . Show that

$$
\int_{0}^{1} \int_{0}^{1} | f(x) + g(y) |^{p} dxdy \geqslant \int_{0}^{1} | f(x) |^{p} dx.
$$

Consider an urn with p plus balls and m minus balls in it, where m and p are given nonnegative numbers. You are allowed to pick a random ball from the urn or quit the game. If you decide to pick and get a plus ball you gain a dollar; if you get a minus ball you lose a dollar. You can continue the game but picked balls are not replaced. Denote by $V(m, p)$ the expected value of playing the game. Find a recurrence for $V(m, p)$

3-1 Let $X_{n}$ be increasing, integrable random variables and converges a.s. to $X \in L^{1}$ , show that, for any sigma algebra $\mathcal{G}, \mathbb{E}(X_{n} \vert \mathcal{G}) \uparrow \mathbb{E}(X \vert \mathcal{G})$

3-2 Let $X_{n} \geq 0$ , Show that

$$
\operatorname{liminf}_{n} \mathbb{E}(X_{n} | \mathcal{G}) \geq \mathbb{E}(\operatorname{liminf}_{n} X_{n} | \mathcal{G}).
$$

3-3 Let $X_{n}$ be random variables in $L^{1}$ and $X_{n} X \mathrm{~ a.s}$ . with $| X_{n} | \leq Z$ in $L^{1}$ . Show that

$$
\operatorname{E}(X | \mathcal{G}) = \lim \operatorname{E}(X_{n} | \mathcal{G}) \text{a.s.and in} L^{1}.
$$

3-4 Show that, if f convex and $X, f(X)$ integrable, then

$$
f(\mathbb{E}(X | \mathcal{G})) \leq \mathbb{E}(f(X | \mathcal{G})).
$$
