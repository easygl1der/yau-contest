## 2013 Probability and Statistics Group Competition Reexamination Questions

1. Let $\xi_{i}\(i=1,2,\cdots)$ be i.i.d. random variables with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma$. Let N be a positive-integer-valued random variable with mean m and variance $\theta$, independent of $(\xi_{i}, i=1,2,\cdots)$. Determine the mean and variance of $X=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\xi_{i}$.

2. If X, Y, and Z are independent random variables, each uniformly distributed on [0,1], prove that $W =(XY)^{Z}$ is also uniformly distributed on [0,1].

3. Use the throwing method to examine the symmetry of a coin, and record the probability of heads as p.

1) Given $n$ independent tosses, derive the maximum likelihood estimator of $p$.

2) When the number of tosses $n$ is large, use asymptotic normality, if desired, to test the hypothesis

$$
H_{0}: p = 0.5, H_{1}: p \neq 0.5,
$$

At significance level $\alpha = 0.10$, give a concrete testing procedure.
