## Probability theory test questions

1. Let $X$ and $Y$ be independent integrable random variables with $E[X] = 0$. Prove that $E|X + Y| \geq E[|Y|]$.

2. Let $X_{1}, X_{2}, \cdots$ be independent random variables on $(0, \infty)$, each with an exponential distribution of rate 1. Define $N(x) = \inf \{n : \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_{i} > x\}$. Determine the expectation of $N(x)$.
