## Probability and Statistics Team Interview Problems

1. Let $X_{1}, X_{2}, \cdots$ be i.i.d. random variables, each $X_{i}$ having an exponential distribution of rate 1. For $x ~ > ~ 0$, define $N(x) \ =$ $\operatorname{inf} \{n : \sum_{i = 1}^{n} X_{i} > x\}$. Determine the expectation of $N(x)$.

2. Let X and Y be independent integrable random variables with $E[X] = 0$. Prove that $E[| X + Y |] \geqslant E[| Y |]$.

3. Test whether a coin is fair by repeated tossing; let p denote its probability of heads.

1) Given n independent tosses, derive the maximum likelihood estimator of p.

2) When n is large, use asymptotic normality, if desired, to test the hypothesis

$$
H_{0}: p = 0.5, H_{1}: p \neq 0.5,
$$

At significance level $\alpha = 0.10$, give a concrete testing procedure.
