## Probability and statistics interview questions (all-around)

1. Let $(\xi_{n})$ be independent random variables satisfying $P(\xi_{n} =$ $1) = p_{n}, P(\xi_{n} = 0) = 1 - p_{n}$. Assuming $\textstyle \sum_{k = 1}^{\infty} p_{k} p_{k + 1} < \infty$, prove that $\scriptstyle \sum_{k = 1}^{\infty} \xi_{k} \xi_{k + 1} \pi$ converges almost surely.

2. Let Z be a standard exponential random variable, so that $P(Z \in dt) =$ $e^{- t} dt$. Define {Z} and [Z] to be the fractional and integer parts of Z, respectively. Prove that {Z} and [Z] are independent, and determine their distributions.
