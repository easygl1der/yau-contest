## Question 1

^yau-2017-final-interview-overall-probability-overall-q1

> Corresponding solution: [Reference solution for Question 1](../../solutions/yau/yau-2017-final-interview-overall-probability-overall-solutions.md#^yau-2017-final-interview-overall-probability-overall-s1).

Let $\epsilon_{1},..., \epsilon_{n},...$ be a sequence of i.i.d. random variables with mean 0 and finite variance $\sigma^{2}$ . Let $x_{1}$ be a constant, and

$$
y_{n} = m(x_{n}) + \epsilon_{n}; x_{n + 1} = x_{n} - cy_{n} / n, \forall n \geq 1,
$$

where c is a positive constant. Suppose that $m(x)$ is smooth and strictly increasing and $m(x^{*}) = 0$ . Show that $x_{n}$ converges to $x^{*}$ in probability.
