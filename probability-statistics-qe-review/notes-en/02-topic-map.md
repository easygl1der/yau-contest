## Order for Further Development

Any fuller review text should first derive a chapter-coverage table from the [[01-syllabus|syllabus]], then develop each topic through motivation, definitions, theorems, complete derivations, worked examples, past-paper solutions, and a pre-examination checklist. Each conclusion should link back to a source paper and cite a nearby textbook or paper.
