Problem 2. Let $(p_{1}, p_{2}, p_{3})$ be the probabilities of a discrete random variable, where $0 < p_{k} < 1$ and $p_{1} + p_{2} +$ $p_{3} = 1$ . Let $r_{1}, r_{2}, r_{3}$ be independent random variables, each following a Unif(0, 1) distribution. Define the random variable X as

$$
X = k, \quad \text{if} r_{k}^{1 / p_{k}} = \max \left\{r_{1}^{1 / p_{1}}, r_{2}^{1 / p_{2}}, r_{3}^{1 / p_{3}} \right\}.
$$

You may ignore the case where the maximum is not unique, as it occurs with probability zero. Determine the distribution of X.
