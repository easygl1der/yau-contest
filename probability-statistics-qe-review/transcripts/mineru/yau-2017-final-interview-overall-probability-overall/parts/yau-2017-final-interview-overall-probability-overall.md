Problem 1. Let $\epsilon _ { 1 } , . . . , \epsilon _ { n } , . . .$ be a sequence of i.i.d. random variables with mean 0 and finite variance $\sigma ^ { 2 }$ . Let $x _ { 1 }$ be a constant, and

$$
y _ {n} = m (x _ {n}) + \epsilon_ {n}; x _ {n + 1} = x _ {n} - c y _ {n} / n, \forall n \geq 1,
$$

where c is a positive constant. Suppose that $m ( x )$ is smooth and strictly increasing and $m ( x ^ { * } ) = 0$ . Show that $x _ { n }$ converges to $x ^ { * }$ in probability.
