## 题 1

^yau-2019-final-interview-overall-prob-personal-overall-q1

> 对应解答：[题 1 参考解](../../solutions/yau/yau-2019-final-interview-overall-prob-personal-overall-solutions.md#^yau-2019-final-interview-overall-prob-personal-overall-s1)。

Let $\{S_{n}\}$ be a simple random walk in one dimension, with $S_{0} = 0$ , and let

$$
\tau = \tau_{[0, 5]^{c}} = \inf \{n: S_{n} \notin[0, 5]\}
$$

be the first time the random walk exits the set {0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5}. Evaluate $\mathbb{E}[S_{\tau - 1}]$ (Hint: use Wald’s identity)

## 题 2

^yau-2019-final-interview-overall-prob-personal-overall-q2

> 对应解答：[题 2 参考解](../../solutions/yau/yau-2019-final-interview-overall-prob-personal-overall-solutions.md#^yau-2019-final-interview-overall-prob-personal-overall-s2)。

Prove that for any two events,

$$
\left| \mathbb{P} \{A \cap B\} - \mathbb{P} \{A\} \mathbb{P} \{B\} \right| \leq \frac{1}{4}
$$

and

$$
\mathbb{P} \{A \cup B\} \mathbb{P} \{A \cap B\} \leq \mathbb{P} \{A\} \mathbb{P} \{B\}.
$$
