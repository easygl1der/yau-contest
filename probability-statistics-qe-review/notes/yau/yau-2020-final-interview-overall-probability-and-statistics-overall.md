## 题 1

^yau-2020-final-interview-overall-probability-and-statistics-overall-q1

> 对应解答：[题 1 参考解](../../solutions/yau/yau-2020-final-interview-overall-probability-and-statistics-overall-solutions.md#^yau-2020-final-interview-overall-probability-and-statistics-overall-s1)。

Let $\left\{X_{n} \right\}$ be a sequence of independent, identically distributed random variables taking values in $\mathbb{N},$ the set of positive integers. Define

$$
R_{n} = \operatorname{Card} \left\{X_{1}, \dots, X_{n} \right\},
$$

i.e., $R_{n}$ is the number of distinct integers in the set $\{X_{1}, \cdots, X_{n}\}$ . Suppose that $\mathbb{E}[X_{1}] < \infty$ . Prove that $R_{n} / \sqrt{n} \to 0$ in probability.

(1) the outcome of $E_{1}$ follows the Binomial model Binomia $(n, \theta)$ with the total number of trials $n > 0$ and the unknown probability of success parameter $\theta \in \left[0, 1 \right]$ , and

(2) the outcome of $E_{2}$ follows the Negative-Binomial model Binomial $(r, \theta)$ with the target for number of successful trials $r > 0$ and the unknown probability of success parameter $\theta \in[0, 1]$

Let H be the observed index to the experiment that is actually conducted, and let X denote the observed outcome of the conducted experiment.

Find a minimal sufficient statistic for θ and prove your claim.
