Note: ln $x : = \log_{e} x.$ $\mathbb{R}$ represents the set of all real numbers. $\mathbb{N} : = \{1, 2, 3,...\}$ denotes the set of all positive integers.


## 题 1（10 分）

Let $X_{1}, X_{2},...$ . be independent identically distributed random variables with density

$$
f(x) = \left\{\begin{array}{ll} | x |^{- 3}, & | x | > 1 \\ 0, & \text{otherwise} \end{array} \right.
$$

and characteristic function $\phi(t) : = \mathbb{E} e^{itX_{1}}$ . Let $S_{n} : = X_{1} + X_{2} + \cdot \cdot \cdot + X_{n}$ for each $n \in \mathbb{N}.$

(a) Prove that $\mathbb{E}(X_{1}^{2}) = \infty$

(b) It is known that $\lim_{t\to0}\frac{1-\phi(t)}{t^2\log|t|}=-1$ you do not need to prove this. Using this fact, find a sequence $(a_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ and prove that $S_{n} / a_{n}$ converges in distribution to the standard normal distribution (i.e., mean 0 and variance 1).


## 题 2（15 分）

Let $X_{1}, X_{2},...$ . be independent identically distributed random variables with exponential distribution: $\mathbb{P}(X_{k} > x) = e^{- x}$ for $x \geq 0$ . Let $M_{n} : = \operatorname{max}_{1 \leq k \leq n} X_{k}$ . Show that

(a) $\limsup_{n\to\infty}X_n/\log n=1$ almost surely.

(b) $\liminf_{n\to\infty}M_n/\log n=1$ almost surely.


## 题 3（10 分）

(a) Prove that if $X_n\xrightarrow{\mathbb{P}}X$ and $Y_n\xrightarrow{\mathbb{P}}Y$, then $X_nY_n\xrightarrow{\mathbb{P}}XY$.

(b) Is the following true or false: if $X_n\xrightarrow{L^1}X$ and $Y_n\xrightarrow{L^1}Y$, then $X_nY_n\xrightarrow{L^1}XY$? Either prove it or give a counterexample.


## 题 4（15 分）

Let $p \in(0, 1)$ and $k \in \mathbb N$ . Let $X_{1}, X_{2}, \cdots$ be independent random variables with $\mathbb{P}(X_n=1) = p = 1 - \mathbb{P}(X_{n} = - 1)$ for each $n \in \mathbb{N}.$ . Let $X_{0} : = 0$ and $S_{n} : = X_{0} + X_{1} + \cdot \cdot \cdot + X_{n}$ for each $n \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{0\}$ . Let $T_{k} : = \operatorname{inf} \{n \in \mathbb{N} : S_{n} = k\}$ with inf $\varnothing = \infty$ . For each $t \in \mathbb{R}$ , let $M(t) : = \mathbb{E} e^{tX_{1}}$

(a) If $p \in[1 / 2, 1)$ , prove that $\mathbb{E}\left[e^{tS_{T_k}}M(t)^{-T_k}\right] = 1$ for all $t > 0$

(b) If $p \in(0, 1 / 2)$ , compute $\mathbb{P}(T_{k} < \infty)$

(c) If $p \in(0, 1 / 2)$ , find the distribution of $Y : = 1 + \operatorname{sup}_{n > 0} S_{n}.$


## 题 5（10 分）

Let $S$ be a countable state space and $(Z_{n}, n \in \mathbb{N})$ be a sequence of independent identically distributed random variables taking values in a measurable space $(E, \mathcal{E})$

(a) Suppose that $f : S \times E \to S$ is a measurable mapping and $X_{0}$ is independent of all $Z_{n}$ Let

$$
X_{n} := f(X_{n - 1}, Z_{n}), n \in \mathbb{N}.\tag{1}
$$

Prove that $(X_{n}, n \in \mathbb{N} \cup \{0\})$ ) is a Markov chain, and determine its transition probability.

(b) Prove that any (discrete) time-homogeneous Markov chain on $S$ can be written in the form (1).


## 题 6（10 分）

Let $\left(B_{t}, t \geq 0 \right)$ be a one-dimensional Brownian motion starting from the origin $(\mathrm{i.e}, B_{0} = 0)$ . Let $\mathcal{F}_{t} : = \sigma(B_{s} : s\le t)$ be the filtration generated by $B_{t}$ . For $a > 0$ , define $T_{a} : = \operatorname{inf} \{t > 0 : B_{t} \geq a\}$

(a) Prove that $T_{a}$ is a $\mathcal{F}_{t}$ stopping time.

(b) Compute $\mathbb{E} e^{- \lambda T_{a}}$ for $\lambda > 0$ , and prove that $\mathbb{P}(T_{a} < \infty) = 1$ . Hint: one may consider $M_{t} : \dot{=} e^{uB_{t} - u^{2} t / 2}.$


## 题 7（15 分）

Let X<sub>i</sub> be independent $\mathrm{N}(\mu_{i}, 1), i = 1,..., n.$ . Let $\boldsymbol{\mu} =(\mu_{1},..., \mu_{n})^{T}, \mathbf{X} =(X_{1},..., X_{n})^{T},$ $\mathbf{a} =(a_{1},..., a_{n})^{T}$ . Consider the following loss function

$$
l(\mu, \mathbf{a}) = \sum_{i = 1}^{n}(a_{i} - \mu_{i})^{2},
$$

prove that $\delta(\mathbf{X}) = \mathbf{X}$ is minimax.


## 题 8（10 分）

Suppose $X_{1}, X_{2},..., X_{n}$ are i.i.d. random variables from $Uniform(\theta, \theta + 10)$ distribution with $\theta > 0$

(a) Prove that $\hat{\theta} = \operatorname{min}(X_{1},..., X_{n})$ is a consistent estimator of $\theta.$

(b) Find the asymptotic distribution of

$$
\sqrt{12n} \left(\frac{\overline{{X}} - 5 - \theta}{\hat{\theta}}\right),
$$

where $\overline{{X}}$ is the sample mean and $\hat{\theta}$ is the consistent estimator for $\theta$ in part (a).

(a) Derive the MLE of $\theta,{\hat{\theta}}.$

(b) What is the asymptotic distribution of ${\hat{\theta}} ?$


## 题 10（10 分）

f(x \mid \theta) = 100^{\theta} \theta x^{-(1 + \theta)}, x > 100,
$$

where $\theta > 1$ . Let $\mu = \operatorname{E}(X)$ be the mean of the income, write down the rejection region of the UMP level α $(0 < \alpha < 1)$ test of testing $H_{0} : \mu = \mu_{0}$ versus $H_{1} : \mu > \mu_{0}$
