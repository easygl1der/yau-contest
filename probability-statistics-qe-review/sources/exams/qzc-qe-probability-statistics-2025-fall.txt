1. (10 points) Let X and N be two independent random variables. Suppose X follows the standard
   exponential distribution (i.e., with probability density function e−x 1{x>0} ), and N follows the
   Poisson distribution with parameter λ > 0.

    (a) Find all λ > 0 such that E[X N ] < ∞.
    (b) Calculate the expectation of E[X N ] under the above condition on λ.
    (c) Is there any λ > 0 such that Var[X N ] < ∞ ? Justify your answer.

    Suppose that the prior on θ is lognormal, i.e., ln θ follows N(µ0 , σ02 ) distribution where µ0 ∈ R
    and σ0 > 0 are known constants.

     (a) Find the posterior density of ln θ.
     (b) Suppose that one defines the Bayes estimate for θ as the value that maximizes the posterior
             density of θ. Find this Bayes estimator. Is it consistent for θ? Please explain your answer.
