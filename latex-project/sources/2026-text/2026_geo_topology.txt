Problem 2. Let (M 2 , g) be a smooth, compact, oriented two–dimensional Riemannian
manifold with nonempty boundary ∂M . Assume that
   (1) the Gaussian curvature satisfies Kg ≥ 0 on M , and
   (2) the geodesic curvature of the boundary satisfies kg ≥ 1 along ∂M . Here kg
       denotes the geodesic curvature of ∂M , computed with respect to the outward
       pointing unit normal along the boundary.
Suppose moreover that
                                 Length(∂M ) ≥ 2π.
Show that (M, g) is isometric to the unit disk (D2 (1), gflat ) equipped with the standard
Euclidean metric.
Problem 3. Let G be a connected n–dimensional Lie group equipped with a bi-invariant
Riemannian metric h·, ·i.
   (1) Show that the sectional curvature of G is nonnegative.
   (2) Assume that the Lie algebra g of G has trivial center, i.e.,
                   z(g) := {X ∈ g | [X, Y ] = 0 for all Y ∈ g} = {0}.
       Show that G is compact.
   (3) Suppose that G is simply connected. Show that G decomposes as a direct product
                                    G∼= G0 × Rk ,
       where G0 is a simply connected compact Lie group whose Lie algebra has trivial
       center, and Rk is the additive Lie group.
