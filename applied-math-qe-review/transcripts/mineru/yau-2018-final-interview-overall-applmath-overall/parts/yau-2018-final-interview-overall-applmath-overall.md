# S.-T. Yau College Student Mathematics Contest, 2018 Applied Mathematics, All-around

Let ${ \bf u } ( t ) = ( u _ { 1 } ( t ) , u _ { 2 } ( t ) , u _ { 3 } ( t ) )$ be a solution of the ODE

$$
{\frac {d \mathbf {u}}{d t}} = \mathbf {a} \times \mathbf {u}.
$$

where × denotes the cross product and $\mathbf { a } = ( a _ { 1 } , a _ { 2 } , a _ { 3 } ) \neq 0$

Consider a forward diference scheme in time

$$
\mathbf {u} ^ {n + 1} = \mathbf {u} ^ {n} + \Delta t (\mathbf {a} \times \mathbf {u} ^ {n}).
$$

1. Show that the scheme is always unstable.

2. Discuss ways to improve the stability of the scheme.
