1. Given a set of column vectors $y_{1}, \cdot \cdot \cdot, y_{n} \in \mathcal{R}^{m}$ , we set $\mathcal{V} = \operatorname{span} \{y_{1}, \cdot \cdot \cdot, y_{n}\} \subset \mathcal{R}^{m}$ . How to find $\ell \le \mathrm{dim} \nu$ orthonormal vectors $\{\psi_{i}\}_{i = 1}^{\ell}$ in ${\mathcal{R}}^{m}$ that minimize

$$
J(\psi_{1}, \dots, \psi_{\ell}) = \sum_{j = 1}^{n} | | y_{j} - \sum_{i = 1}^{\ell}(y_{j}^{T} \psi_{i}) \psi_{i} | |^{2}
$$

with the Euclidean norm $| | y | | = \sqrt{y^{T} y}$
