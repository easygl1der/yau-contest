## Spring, 2026


## Problem 1 (10 points)

Assume that locally the function $f \in C^{2}(\mathbb{R})$ has a unique zero $x_*$ with $f^{\prime}(x_{\star}) \neq 0$ . Consider the iteration

$$
x_{k + 1} = x_{k} - \frac{[f(x_{k})]^{2}}{f(x_{k} + f(x_{k})) - f(x_{k})}, k = 0, 1, \dots.
$$

Prove that $x_{k} \to x_{\star}$ , and estimate its order of local convergence.


## Problem 2 (20 points)

For any continuous function $f,$ define $\langle f, g \rangle = \int_{- 1}^{1} f(x) g(x)$ dx and $\| f \|_{2} ={\sqrt{\langle f, f \rangle}}$ . Write $a_{n} =$ ${\frac{(2n) !}{2^{n}(n !)^{2}}}.$

Define the n-th monic Legendre polynomial by: $P_{0}(x) = 1, P_{1}(x) = x, P_{n + 1}(x) = xP_{n}(x) - \frac{n^{2}}{4n^{2} - 1} P_{n - 1}(x).$

(a) Compute $\langle P_{n}, P_{m} \rangle$ , for $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$

(b) Prove that $P_{n}$ solves $\min_{\substack{p\in\mathcal{P}_n\p^{(n)}(1)=n!}}\|p\|_2$ and compute the minimal value.

(c) For $f \in C^{n + 1}[- 1, 1]$ , choose the interpolation nodes $x_{0}, x_{1}, \ldots, x_{n}$ as the $n + 1$ zeros of $P_{n + 1}(x)$ , and denote its degree-n interpolation polynomial by $L_{n}$ . Prove

$$
\| f - L_{n} \|_{2} \leq \frac{2 \| f^{(n + 1)} \|_{2}}{(2n + 3) a_{n + 1}(n + 1) !}.
$$

(d) For $f \in C^{2n + 2}[- 1, 1]$ , derive the Gauss quadrature formula o $I(f) = \int_{- 1}^{1} f(x) d x \mathrm{for} n + 1$ quadrature nodes, written by $I_{n + 1}(f)$ , and estimate its error $| I(f) - I_{n + 1}(f) |$


## Problem 3 (20 points)

Given $A \in \mathbb{R}^{l \times m}, B \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times p}, C \in \mathbb{R}^{l \times p}$ . Consider the problem $\operatorname{min}_{X} \| AXB - C \|_{\mathrm{F}}$ , where $\lVert \cdot \rVert_{\mathrm{F}}$ is the Frobenius norm of a matrix.

(a) Prove that the minimal value is $0,$ if and only if $\operatorname{rank}(A) = \operatorname{rank} \left(\left[A \quad C \right] \right), \operatorname{rank}(B)=\operatorname{rank}\left(\begin{bmatrix}B\C\end{bmatrix}\right)$

(b) Show that there exists a unique minimal point $X_{\star},$ if and only if \operatorname{rank}(A)=m,\ operatorname{rank}(B)=n.

(c) Propose a numerical method to compute the unique X<sub>⋆</sub> in (b).

(d) Does $X_{\star}$ also minimize $\| AXB - C \|_{2} ?$ Why? Here $\lVert \cdot \rVert_{2}$ is the spectral norm of a matrix.


## Problem 4 (15 points)

For the advection equation $u_{t} + u_{x} = 0$ , analyze the stability of the implicit schemes

$$
\mathrm{(a)} \frac{u_{j}^{n + 1} - u_{j}^{n}}{\tau} + \frac{u_{j}^{n + 1} - u_{j - 1}^{n + 1}}{h} = 0;
$$

$$
\mathrm{(b)} \frac{u_{j}^{n + 1} - u_{j}^{n}}{\tau} + \frac{u_{j + 1}^{n + 1} - u_{j}^{n + 1}}{h} = 0;
$$

$$
\mathrm{(c)} \frac{u_{j}^{n + 1} - u_{j}^{n}}{\tau} + \frac{u_{j + 1}^{n + 1} - u_{j - 1}^{n + 1}}{2h} = 0.
$$


## Problem 5 (15 points)

Write and prove the maximum principle of the centered finite diference scheme for discretizing the equation

$$
u_{xx} + u_{yy} + du_{x} + eu_{y} + fu = 0, \quad f < 0,
$$

under some suitable assumptions. Apply the maximum principle to show the stability of the backward Euler scheme for the parabolic equation

$$
u_{t} -(u_{xx} + u_{yy} + du_{x} + eu_{y} + fu) = 0, \quad(x, y) \in(0, 1) \times(0, 1),
$$

with homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions.

You can choose either 6 or 7 to answer. The points will be decided as max(6, 7).


## Problem 6 (20 points)

Consider the following system

$$
\ddot{x} - 2x - x^{2} + x^{3} = 0.
$$

(a) (6 points) Find the static solutions to the system.

(b) (14 points) For suficiently small but nonzero ϵ, show that the orbit starting at $(x(0), \dot{x}(0)) =(2 + \epsilon, 0)$ is closed in the phase space, i.e., x − x˙ plane. Find the approximation of the period of this orbit up to $O(\epsilon^2)$ .


## Problem 7 (20 points)

The polar of $C \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{n}$ is defined as the set

$$
C^{\circ} = \left\{y \in \mathbb{R}^{n} \mid y^{T} x \leq 1 \text{for all} x \in C \right\}.
$$

(a) (2 points) Show that $C^{\circ}$ is convex (even if C is not).

(b) (3 points) What is the polar of a cone?

(c) (3 points) What is the polar of the unit ball for a norm $\| \cdot \| ?$

(d) (4 points) What is the polar of the set $C = \{x \mid \mathbf{1}^{T} x = 1, x \succeq 0\} \ ?$

(e) (8 points) Show that if C is closed and convex, with $0 \in C$ , then $\left(C^{\circ} \right)^{\circ} = C$
