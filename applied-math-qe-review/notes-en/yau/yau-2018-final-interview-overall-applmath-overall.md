Let ${\bf u}(t) =(u_{1}(t), u_{2}(t), u_{3}(t))$ be a solution of the ODE

$$
{\frac{d \mathbf{u}}{dt}} = \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{u}.
$$

where × denotes the cross product and $\mathbf{a} =(a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}) \neq 0$

Consider a forward diference scheme in time

$$
\mathbf{u}^{n + 1} = \mathbf{u}^{n} + \Delta t(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{u}^{n}).
$$
