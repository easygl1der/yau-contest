## (Applied Math. of TEAM)

Given a region $\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}^{3}$ , please deduce the partial diferential equations of the fluid movement according to the conservation law of the mass and momentum.

Assume $\rho = \rho(t, \boldsymbol{x})$ is the density, $u =(u_{1}, u_{2}, u_{3})(u_{i} = u_{i}(t, x))$ is the velocity(speed) of the fluid, and $p = p(t, \boldsymbol{x})$ is the pressure, where $\boldsymbol{x} =(x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3})$ is the space variables.

Question 1: Given two diferent expression of the change of the mass in Ω of the fluid with the density $\rho(t, x)$ within the time interval $[t_{1}, t_{2}]$

Question 2: Please deduce the partial diferential equations of $\rho, u$ according to the conservation of the mass in Ω.

Question 3: Assume there is no outside force acting on the fluid in Ω, please give two diferent expression of the change of the momentum of the fluid with the density $\rho(t, x)$ and the velocity $u(t, x)$ in Ω within the time interval $[t_{1}, t_{2}]$

Question 4: According to the conservation of the momentum of the fluid in Ω, deduce the partial diferential equation of $\rho, u, p$
