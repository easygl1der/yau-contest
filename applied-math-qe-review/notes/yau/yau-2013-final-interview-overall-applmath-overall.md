Let $D_{n} = \{x \in \mathbb{R}^{n} : | x | < 1\}$ , f be a smooth map from $\overline{{D_{n}}}$ to $\bar{D}_{n}$ . For homotopy $H : \overline{{{D_{n}}}} \times[0, 1] \to \mathbb{R}^{n}$

$$
H(x, t) =(1 - t)(x - a) + t(x - f(x))
$$

we can find $x = f(x)$ from $H(x, t) = 0$ . Here $a \in D_{n}$
