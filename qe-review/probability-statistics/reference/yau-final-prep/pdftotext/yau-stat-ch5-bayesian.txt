5.3 Bayesian Inference
Definition 5.4 (Bayesian Point Estimation). Common Bayesian point estimators:
                                        ∫
  1. Posterior Mean: θ̂P M = E[θ|x] = θπ(θ|x)dθ
    2. Posterior Median: The 50th percentile of the posterior distribution
    3. Maximum A Posteriori (MAP): θ̂M AP = arg maxθ π(θ|x)
Definition 5.5 (Credible Intervals). A 100(1 − α)% credible interval for θ is an interval
[L, U ] such that:
                               P (θ ∈ [L, U ]|x) = 1 − α
    Types of credible intervals:
    1. Equal-tailed: P (θ < L|x) = P (θ > U |x) = α/2
    2. Highest Posterior Density (HPD): Shortest interval containing 100(1 − α)%
       of posterior probability
Theorem 5.5 (Bayesian Decision Theory). Let δ(x) be a decision rule and L(θ, δ) be a
loss function. The Bayes risk is:
                                      ∫
                              rπ (δ) = ρ(π, δ(x))m(x)dx
                  ∫
where ρ(π, δ(x)) = L(θ, δ(x))π(θ|x)dθ is the posterior expected loss.
   The Bayes estimator minimizes the Bayes risk.
   Common loss functions:
    1. Squared Error Loss: L(θ, δ) = (θ − δ)2 ⇒ Bayes estimator = Posterior mean
    2. Absolute Error Loss: L(θ, δ) = |θ − δ| ⇒ Bayes estimator = Posterior median
    3. 0-1 Loss: L(θ, δ) = 1{θ̸=δ} ⇒ Bayes estimator = Posterior mode
