# Peng Ding — *A First Course in Causal Inference*

[← Probability & Statistics references](../README.md) · [Provenance](./SOURCE.md) · [arXiv:2305.18793](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18793)

Berkeley STAT causal-inference notes, later the English monograph. Author: Peng Ding, Department of Statistics, UC Berkeley.

This is the **full book** on the QE shelf, copied from Mac Mini `aca-workflow/PDFs/causal-inference/`. It is **not** an official Qiuzhen syllabus book. Use it when a past paper or personal review needs Neyman–Rubin potential outcomes, randomization inference, IPW / propensity scores, IV, or RDD.

The linear-model companion on this shelf is [`../ding-linear-model/`](../ding-linear-model/README.md).
