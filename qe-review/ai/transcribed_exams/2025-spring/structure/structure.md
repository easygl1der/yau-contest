## Recurring dependencies

1. Translate the prompt into its mathematical or systems object before memorizing a result.
2. Keep assumptions visible: sampling regime, optimization regularity, model factorization, or deployment constraint.
3. For derivations, identify the reusable lemma and the exact quantity that must be conditioned on, bounded, or optimized.
4. For design questions, specify data flow, objective, evaluation, and failure handling—not just component names.
